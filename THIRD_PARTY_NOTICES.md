## KokonutUI Gradient Button

Source: https://kokonutui.com/r/gradient-button.json

Retrieved with the configured shadcn MCP, `get_item_examples_from_registries`,
query `gradient-button`, registry `@kokonutui`, on 2026-09-04.
Upstream component: @dorianbaffier, version 1.0.0, dated 2025-06-26.

The locally vendored component is adapted for static Astro rendering and anchor
navigation. The emerald layers are adapted to the dark theme; the label is opaque,
transitions are shortened, reduced motion is respected, and the local shadcn
Button dependency is replaced by a semantic anchor. This is source integration,
not installation of the entire KokonutUI library or its demos.
